01 / ABOUT

Your plans.
A clear way forward.

I’m Esko Savioja, a licensed real estate agent at Kuivinen LKV. I help you navigate selling, buying and moving home in the Helsinki metropolitan area.

For sellers, I focus on realistic pricing and a clear plan. If you are looking to buy, I can help you understand the process and the questions to ask. When a move involves both selling and buying, we can look at how the timings fit together. You are welcome to get in touch while you are still exploring your options.

02 / A SHARED UNDERSTANDING

A musician who understands real estate

I have worked as a professional musician for decades, including at the Finnish National Opera since 2003. International colleagues and the rhythms of musical life have long been part of my everyday world.

That experience helps me understand the practical questions behind a move: settling into a new country, balancing work commitments and deciding whether a home will suit the way you live. My service is equally open to international professionals in other fields.

Whether you are moving to Finland or already live here, I take time to understand your priorities. For musicians, that may include space to practise, travel commitments or the distance to rehearsals. For other professionals, the details may be different; the starting point is the same: a home that works for your life.

My approach is calm and analytical: I listen, explain the options and give a realistic view of pricing and the steps involved. Trust matters more to me than pressure.
